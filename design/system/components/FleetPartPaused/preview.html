What can delay a job, and when it asks again
  • A pause windowMoved to the window's end; never dropped, reserves nothing.
  • Retry backoffAfter an infrastructure failure, forge and local jobs try once more, from 60 seconds and growing.
  • A busy scopeOne local job per folder, or a scoped limit's at-once count: asks again every 5 seconds.
  • A busy model endpointIts concurrency in model-endpoints.json is full: asks again every 7 seconds.
  • Host slots fullPI_CONCURRENCY is taken: asks again every 5 seconds.
  • The host budgetIts size fits the host, just not now: asks again about every 9 seconds and keeps its place.
  • Never fits this hostA shared job larger than this host's budget is put back for 60 seconds for another host.
