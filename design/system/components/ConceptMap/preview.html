How pi-dispatch fits together

One picture of the pieces the cards draw, and where a person steps in.

The map

left to right: what starts work, how it runs, how it ends, and where you come in

What starts work

  • Triggers: cron, label, comment, issue and pull request rules, written by hand.
  • The CLI: pi-dispatch run, from a shell.
  • Watch rules: a finding in logs or alerts starts a read-only job. planned, #735
  • Dispatchers: start listed jobs and propose triggers. proposed

Queue and host

  • The queue holds each job until a slot, a time or a check is free.
  • A worker on a host takes it, after the caps are checked.
  • A venue (local, podman or openshell) runs one job in one container.

The run

  • It becomes a run with a record: outcome, reason, tokens and cost.
  • Completed, failed or refused, always with its word.
  • Parked: it ended to wait for an answer.
  • Waiting for approval of a named action. planned, #649

Where a person steps in

  • Steer or cancel while it runs.
  • Merge the pull request: always yours, never automatic.
  • Open its sandbox: the kept workspace, no credentials.
  • Re-run, with the cost previewed first.
  • Answer the question; the run resumes.
  • Send a drafted answer from an answer rule. proposed, #753
  • Decide the approval. planned, #649

What is counted

  • Spend against caps: every job reserves before it calls a model.
  • Touches: each run a person acted on, in Zero touch. proposed, #753
  • Solid: in pi-dispatch today
  • Dashed blue: planned, with its issue
  • Dashed grey: proposed, nothing decided
  • Violet edge: a person acts here
  • Tinted: how a run ends, always with its word
