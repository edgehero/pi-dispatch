Buttons by kind of act, and the two-step confirm bar.

One solid pill per card for the step forward. Answer is violet and only for parked runs. Paid acts (re-run, retry, adopt a model) are outlined in yellow, destructive acts (pause, cancel, remove, decline) in red, until the confirm bar, where the confirm button fills. Every action carries an agent capability and reason: an agent may act alone, needs a person to confirm, or only a person may act. Evaluated transitions and Undo keep the same boundary. Every confirm bar lists what will happen, an Undo line and the preview ticket's expiry, and opens below the card that asked, never inside a table cell.
